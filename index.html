Install

Word on Windows

Download and run the installer, then restart Word. No admin rights needed.

Download installer Uninstaller

Windows may warn about a downloaded script: choose “More info → Run anyway”. It only downloads the manifest below and registers it for Word.

Word on Mac

  1. Download manifest-github.xml.
  2. In Finder, press ⇧⌘G and open ~/Library/Containers/com.microsoft.Word/Data/Documents/wef (create the wef folder if missing).
  3. Copy the manifest there and restart Word.

Word for the web

  1. Download manifest-github.xml.
  2. In Word, open Insert → Add-ins → More Settings → Upload My Add-in, and choose the file.

Already installed an earlier version? Run the installer again (Mac/web: replace the manifest) and restart Word to get the two panes.

Set up the three columns

  1. Open References → Left source and References → Right source.
  2. Both open on the right. Drag the Left source pane by its title bar to the left edge of the Word window.
  3. Widen both panes as you like. Word remembers the layout.

The left pane shows the citation at (or just after) the cursor, the right pane the next one. Reading forward, sources move from right to left. Pin 📌 a pane to keep its source while the other one follows the cursor.

Use

  1. Click Add folder in either pane and pick the folder with your source PDFs. Both panes use them, and they are remembered.
  2. Make each citation a hyperlink to its source: the PDF file, or its doi.org address.
  3. Attach a Word comment to the citation saying what you cite from it.
  4. Move the cursor in the text. The citations near it open on both sides.

Writing the comment

Main evidence for the leaching claim:
[key] "Autumn application of cattle slurry increased nitrate leaching by 38 percent"
[rel] "On organic soils the leaching losses were nearly twice those of clay soils"
They also recommend timing manure by soil frost instead of calendar dates.
[key] "text" or just "text"strong + underline key part
[rel] "text"medium relevant
[some] "text"light some relevance
other free textmatching sentences found automatically, shown medium or light

The colour (turquoise or green) tells which citation a passage belongs to; the strength tells how important it is. Quotes don’t need to be exact. Automatic matching compares words, so use quotes when the comment and source are in different languages.
